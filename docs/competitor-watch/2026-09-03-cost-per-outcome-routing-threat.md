# Competitor watch — cost-per-outcome routing convergence

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: MATERIAL THREAT / POSITIONING CONSTRAINT

## Fresh evidence

A fresh public-market scan found multiple products/content layers now explicitly using cost-per-success or cost-per-outcome as the routing/evaluation metric. Most materially, TheRouter.ai published a cost-per-outcome routing framework in August 2026 that compares systems using attempts required to pass a quality bar and total cost per successful task. Evaltude separately documents cost per successful result as cost/request divided by success rate. EvalRig publishes cost-per-passed-run benchmarks.

Sources:
- https://therouter.ai/blog/cost-per-outcome-routing-framework-reference/
- https://evaltude.com/docs/learn/perf-cost-success/
- https://evalrig.ai/benchmarks/frontier-3way/

## Decision

Do NOT claim that `cost per successful result`, `cost per outcome`, or outcome-aware routing by itself is unique to Outcome Economics Intelligence.

James's defensible initial claim must remain narrower and evidence-bound:

> For a specified structured-search task, run request-equivalent paid trials across eligible providers, record actual attempts and settled spend, apply a deterministic acceptance test, and rank the observed providers by effective cost per accepted result.

Until paid benchmark evidence exists, this is a planned capability rather than a proven commercial claim.

## Commercial implication

The A$49 founding pilot should sell the *measured cross-provider decision artifact*, not the generic metric. The deliverable must show:
1. frozen task/request-equivalence definition;
2. providers actually tested;
3. paid attempt/settlement evidence;
4. deterministic PASS/FAIL criteria;
5. observed retries and latency;
6. effective cost per accepted result;
7. evidence limitations and timestamp.

## Kill/reposition trigger

Escalate for kill/reposition review if a credible competitor offers the same request-equivalent, real-paid, deterministic cross-provider structured-search benchmark as a low-friction purchasable service at comparable or lower price, and James cannot demonstrate a materially better evidence artifact, workflow, distribution advantage, or recurring decision value.

## Controls

No customer payment or delivery promise before independent Relay records `APPROVED_FOR_LIMITED_PILOT`. No synthetic evidence may be represented as paid or observed. No signer funding until the isolated no-secret signer boundary passes the required security gate.
