# Demand evidence policy — 29 Aug 2026

Outcome Economics Intelligence must not treat raw x402 settlement/call counts as equivalent to independent market demand.

## Evidence correction
A July 2026 population-scale study of x402 activity reported extreme concentration and found that a substantial share of settlements were fictitious or internal to linked clusters. Therefore transaction count alone is manufacturable and is not sufficient proof of external demand.

## James policy
Rank demand evidence in this order:
1. independently attributable payer diversity or distinct third-party buyers;
2. repeated external purchases tied to identifiable endpoints and independent payer identities;
3. off-chain marketplace buyer/review evidence;
4. raw transaction/call counts only as a weak corroborating signal.

Raw settlement count must never be promoted into success probability, deterministic task acceptance, or effective cost per acceptable result.

This does not invalidate previously recorded marketplace observations; it changes their evidentiary weight. Existing records with payer counts remain useful only where the payer measure is independently derived and not merely transaction volume.
